# 🧪 Generative AI for Productivity — Hands-On Labs

**Program:** L0-FGP · Generative AI for Productivity (الذكاء الاصطناعي التوليدي لرفع الإنتاجية)
**Delivered through:** [SDAIA Academy](https://github.com/SDAIAAcademy)

Nine practical labs and one final project. **No coding.** You'll use real generative AI tools the way you would at work.

---

## 🛠️ What you need

| Need | Notes |
|---|---|
| A browser with **two tabs** | Tab 1: this lab · Tab 2: your AI tool |
| **One AI chat tool** of your choice | [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) · [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) · [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com) · [Claude](https://claude.ai). Free versions are fine |
| A **personal Google account** | For Gemini Notebook, Gems, and the Drive connector lab |
| A **GitHub account** | For your final project. [Create it before Day 1](project/GITHUB_QUICKSTART.md) |

> 🗳️ **Pick one chat tool and stay with it.** Your trainer will compare results across tools for the whole class.

## 🧭 How each lab works

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 📋 | **Ready-made prompt.** Click the copy button, paste it into your AI tool |
| ✏️ | **Your turn.** Write or change the prompt yourself |
| 👀 | **Observe** and compare |
| 🔒 | **Hidden answer.** Try first, then click to reveal |
| 🎥 | **Trainer demo** of a feature that needs an eligible account or licence |
| 💼 / 🎓 | Choose your track: **employed** or **preparing for a job** |

## 🔐 Golden rules

- All names, numbers and companies here are **fictional**.
- **Never** paste real personal data, passwords, or confidential work documents into a public AI tool.
- Connect AI tools only to **test folders** during training, and **remove access** when you're done.
- AI output is a **draft**. You are responsible for the final result.
