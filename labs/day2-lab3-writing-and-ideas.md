## Round 1 · One message, three audiences 🎭

**The news:** *"Deadline moved to Friday, budget of SAR 250,000 approved, need sign-off by end of day."*

Run each prompt in a **new chat**.

📋 **For your manager**
```text
Act as a professional communications assistant. Write an email to my manager with these facts: the project deadline has moved to this Friday, the budget of SAR 250,000 has been approved, and I need their final sign-off by end of day today. Tone: formal and concise; lead with the decision I need. Under 80 words, with a subject line.
```

📋 **For your team**
```text
Act as a professional communications assistant. Write a message to my team with these facts: the project deadline has moved to this Friday, the budget of SAR 250,000 has been approved, and we are waiting for final sign-off today. Tone: warm and upbeat; explain what changes for them this week. Under 120 words.
```

📋 **For the client**
```text
Act as a professional communications assistant. Write an email to our client with these facts: the delivery deadline has moved to this Friday and the project budget has been approved internally. Tone: polite, reassuring and professional; avoid internal jargon and do not mention internal sign-offs. Under 100 words, with a subject line.
```

👀 **Observe**
- What did the AI put **first** in each version?
- Why does the client prompt say *"do not mention internal sign-offs"*?

<details>
<summary>🔒 <b>What you should notice</b></summary>

- **Manager:** leads with the request (sign-off).
- **Team:** leads with what changes for them.
- **Client:** reassures them and hides internal details. The client doesn't need to know about internal approvals.

The facts barely changed. **The audience changed the message.**

</details>

## Round 2 · Fix the tone 🧯

A colleague wants to send this message. It's a bit too blunt. 😬

📋
```text
أعد صياغة الرسالة التالية بأسلوب مهذّب ومهني، مع الحفاظ على نفس المطلوب. أقل من 60 كلمة.

الرسالة: "للمرة الثالثة أطلب منكم التقرير. أرسلوه اليوم ولا تتأخروا مرة ثانية."
```

📋 Then, in the same chat:
```text
Now give me the same message in English, suitable for an email to a senior colleague.
```

👀 **Observe:** The request stayed the same (send the report today), but the relationship stayed intact. 🤝

## Round 3 · Brainstorm like a pro 💡

Stay in the **same chat** for all three steps.

📋 **Step 1: go wide**
```text
Act as a workplace productivity consultant. Our team of 10 spends about 12 hours a week in meetings, and many feel the meetings are too long. Give me 10 different ideas to reduce meeting time without hurting communication. One line per idea.
```

📋 **Step 2: narrow down**
```text
Pick the 3 ideas that are easiest to start next week. For each one, give one advantage and one risk.
```

📋 **Step 3: challenge it**
```text
Play devil's advocate: what is the biggest reason these 3 ideas might fail in a Saudi workplace, and how could we prevent it?
```

👀 **Observe:** Step 1 gives **quantity**, Step 2 gives **focus**, and Step 3 gives **critical thinking**. This 3-step pattern works for almost any problem.

## 🧠 Quick quiz

**Q1.** You need to email a client about a delay. Which instruction matters most?
A) Add emojis  B) Include all internal details  C) Polite, reassuring tone without internal jargon  D) Make it as long as possible

<details><summary>🔒 Answer</summary>

**C.** Clients need clarity and reassurance, not your internal process.

</details>

**Q2.** What is the best order for brainstorming with AI?
A) Ask for 1 idea and use it  B) Go wide → narrow down → challenge  C) Challenge first, then go wide  D) Only ask for pros

<details><summary>🔒 Answer</summary>

**B. Go wide → narrow down → challenge.**

</details>
